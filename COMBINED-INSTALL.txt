TABS Fusion + Hit Replay v0.6.3

中文教程：https://github.com/swaggyp7/tabs-order-tablet/blob/main/docs/HIT-REPLAY.md

订阅并启用 TABS Vehicle Framework、TABS Fusion 和 TABS Hit Replay。
1. 退出游戏。备份 TABS 目录中的 main.lua 和 TABS/scripts/ballistics.lua。
2. 本包使用的原版基础文件 SHA256：
main.lua: 84dc0a005180e8c83accbe7aad6cc9548d985912b6070c4c3a6033c04bda4994
TABS/scripts/ballistics.lua: 9536edc20189cadd5c0f91c1c77a5820a5062772e7223a8f242fa2a6aac599f2
已安装支持版本补丁时，可对照 checksums.json 中的 supported_hit_replay_*、supported_fusion_main_sha256 和对应输出文件哈希。其他版本或未知修改请勿覆盖。
3. 将 TABS-bridge 文件夹里的内容复制进 SteamLibrary/steamapps/workshop/content/1167630/3148138736/，保持目录结构，替换两个文件：
   TABS-bridge/main.lua -> 3148138736/main.lua
   TABS-bridge/TABS/scripts/ballistics.lua -> 3148138736/TABS/scripts/ballistics.lua
4. 工坊已订阅 Fusion 时不用再装本地副本；否则复制 tabs-vehicle-recorder 到 Documents/Teardown/mods。Hit Replay 界面需单独订阅，本 ZIP 不含其工具本体。
5. 启用三个模组，重新进入单人地图。
6. Fusion 检测到回放桥接后，使用 F6 全部执行；F7 当前车、F5 停止当前车。Hit Replay 保留 F8 开关、F9 重播、F10 目标模式。工具界面会显示 F6。Backspace 撤销指令时也会关闭当前回放叠层。

本包已合并桥接。不要再用 Fusion 普通版或独立 Hit Replay 替换包覆盖其中一个文件。
从普通版升级应同时替换两个文件。卸载或还原时恢复你自己备份的两个文件；备份若已有某个补丁，恢复后会回到该补丁状态。
TABS 更新后需重新检查兼容性。

ENGLISH
Subscribe to TABS Vehicle Framework, TABS Fusion and TABS Hit Replay. Close Teardown and back up BOTH main.lua and TABS/scripts/ballistics.lua in the TABS framework directory. Verify compatibility using checksums.json. Copy the CONTENTS of TABS-bridge into SteamLibrary/steamapps/workshop/content/1167630/3148138736/, preserving subdirectories and replacing both files.
Use either the Workshop Fusion tool or the included local tool folder, not both. The Hit Replay UI must be subscribed separately. Enable all three mods and reload a single-player level.
Fusion uses F6 for START ALL when the replay bridge is detected. F7 starts selected; F5 stops selected. Hit Replay keeps F8 toggle, F9 replay, F10 target mode. Backspace also dismisses the active replay overlay.
Do not apply standalone bridge replacements on top of this combined pair. To uninstall, restore YOUR backups of BOTH files. Recheck compatibility after TABS updates.
